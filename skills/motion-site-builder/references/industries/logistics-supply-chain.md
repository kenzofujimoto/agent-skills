# Logística e supply chain / Logistics & Supply Chain

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `logistics-supply-chain`
- **Grupo:** Mobilidade, indústria e infraestrutura
- **Subnichos:** ocean freight, air freight, last mile, freight forwarding, autonomous delivery
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 3–7/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `operational`

## Objetivos frequentes

- make network visibility tangible
- drive quote or shipment
- explain reliability
- show global reach

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- services
- network map
- tracking
- quotes
- operational visibility
- industry solutions

## Metáforas de movimento adequadas

- routes connecting
- cargo progression
- network status
- handoff sequence
- warehouse flow

## Arquétipos recomendados

- `map-explorer`
- `tracking-portal`
- `quote-flow`
- `network-storytelling`

## Engines e mídia

**Engines:** maps, DOM/SVG, GSAP for network story, Motion

**Mídia:**

- route maps
- fleet imagery
- tracking UI
- warehouse visuals

## Conversão

- get quote
- track shipment
- contact expert
- explore network

## Evitar

- decorative maps without utility
- invented ETAs
- motion blocking tracking
- too much WebGL on operational flows
