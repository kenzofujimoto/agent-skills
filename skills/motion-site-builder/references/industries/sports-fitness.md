# Esportes e fitness / Sports & Fitness

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `sports-fitness`
- **Grupo:** Saúde, educação e cultura
- **Subnichos:** sportswear, training apps, fitness hardware, clubs, performance analytics
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 5–8/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `health-and-performance`

## Objetivos frequentes

- create energy and identity
- show performance value
- drive membership or commerce

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- product or program

- metrics
- training
- community
- plans

- motion-induced nausea
- unsafe training claims
- video masking product info
- ignoring reduced motion
