# Infantil e família / Kids & Family

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `kids-family`
- **Grupo:** Consumo e estilo de vida
- **Subnichos:** toys, audio players, parenting products, learning kits, family services
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 5–8/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `child-safety`

## Objetivos frequentes

- delight both child and parent
- explain age fit and safety
- drive commerce or subscription

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- age ranges
- safety
- how it works
- parent controls
- learning value
- products

## Metáforas de movimento adequadas

- playful physics
- character states
- story progression
- object discovery
- reward feedback

## Arquétipos recomendados

- `product-explorer`
- `ecommerce`
- `subscription`
- `interactive-story`

## Engines e mídia

**Engines:** Rive, Motion, CSS, light Three.js

**Mídia:**

- illustration
- real product imagery
- characters
- short demonstrations

## Conversão

- shop
- choose age
- subscribe
- explore stories

## Evitar

- overstimulation
- sound without consent
- tiny controls
- dark patterns toward children
