# Companhias aéreas e operações de viagem / Airlines & Travel Operations

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `airlines-travel-operations`
- **Grupo:** Mobilidade, indústria e infraestrutura
- **Subnichos:** airlines, airports, loyalty programs, flight operations, premium travel
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 2–6/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `transactional`

## Objetivos frequentes

- enable booking
- communicate service tiers
- inspire destinations
- support disruption handling

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- search and booking
- routes
- cabins
- baggage
- status
- accessibility
- loyalty

## Metáforas de movimento adequadas

- route drawing
- destination transitions
- cabin reveals
- journey progression

## Arquétipos recomendados

- `booking-flow`
- `destination-explorer`
- `loyalty-portal`
- `service-status`

## Engines e mídia

**Engines:** DOM-first, Motion, maps, light GSAP for inspiration pages

**Mídia:**

- destination photography
- route maps
- cabin imagery
- service UI

## Conversão

- search flights
- manage trip
- join loyalty
- upgrade cabin

## Evitar

- motion slowing booking
- hiding fees or baggage rules
- accessibility barriers
- autoplay sound
