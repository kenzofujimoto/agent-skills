# Pets / Pets

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `pets`
- **Grupo:** Consumo e estilo de vida
- **Subnichos:** pet food, accessories, insurance, health, tracking
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 4–7/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `commerce-and-care`

## Objetivos frequentes

- build warmth and trust
- explain fit or nutrition
- drive subscription or commerce

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- product fit
- ingredients
- pet outcomes
- size guidance
- subscription
- reviews

## Metáforas de movimento adequadas

- playful character motion
- product assembly
- pet journey
- tracking map
- gentle microinteractions

## Arquétipos recomendados

- `ecommerce-product`
- `subscription`
- `quiz`
- `pet-profile`

## Engines e mídia

**Engines:** Motion, Rive, light GSAP, Three.js for product

**Mídia:**

- real pet photography
- product imagery
- illustration
- tracking UI

## Conversão

- shop
- build plan
- subscribe
- check compatibility

## Evitar

- overly childish UI for serious health
- anthropomorphic distraction
- missing safety/size guidance
