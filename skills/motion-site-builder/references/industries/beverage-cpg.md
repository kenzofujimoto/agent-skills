# Bebidas e bens de consumo / Beverage & CPG

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `beverage-cpg`
- **Grupo:** Consumo e estilo de vida
- **Subnichos:** soft drinks, functional beverages, coffee, snacks, packaged goods
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 5–9/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `brand-and-commerce`

## Objetivos frequentes

- build brand memorability
- show product range
- support e-commerce and retail discovery

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- products
- flavors
- ingredients
- brand voice
- where to buy
- subscriptions

## Metáforas de movimento adequadas

- liquid behavior
- packaging rotation
- flavor worlds
- bold type
- playful interactions

## Arquétipos recomendados

- `brand-world`
- `ecommerce-product`
- `product-launch`
- `store-locator`

## Engines e mídia

**Engines:** GSAP, Motion, Three.js for packaging, shader/liquid effect selectively

**Mídia:**

- pack photography
- 3D packaging
- ingredient imagery
- campaign content

## Conversão

- shop
- find store
- subscribe
- explore flavors

## Evitar

- illegible brand play
- constant cursor gimmicks
- fake liquid physics hurting performance
- missing nutritional info

assets, textos, layout exato ou assumir que a tecnologia observada anteriormente permanece igual.
