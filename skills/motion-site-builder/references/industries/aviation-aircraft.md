# Aviação e aeronaves / Aviation & Aircraft

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `aviation-aircraft`
- **Grupo:** Mobilidade, indústria e infraestrutura
- **Subnichos:** commercial aircraft, business aviation, eVTOL, aircraft manufacturing, maintenance
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 4–8/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `safety-accuracy`

## Objetivos frequentes

- show engineering and range
- communicate safety and cabin experience
- support sales
- present fleet

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- aircraft family
- range maps
- cabin
- performance
- engineering
- support network

## Metáforas de movimento adequadas

- flight path
- cutaway reveal
- cabin traversal
- wing airflow
- altitude and range

## Arquétipos recomendados

- `fleet-explorer`
- `3d-product-viewer`
- `range-map`
- `sales-lead-generation`

## Engines e mídia

**Engines:** Three.js/R3F for licensed models, GSAP, map visualization, Motion

**Mídia:**

- aircraft 3D
- cutaways
- cabin panoramas
- range maps
- technical data

## Conversão

- configure cabin
- contact sales
- compare aircraft
- locate support

## Evitar

- fictional performance data
- unlicensed aircraft models
- overly game-like controls
- motion that implies unsafe behavior
