# Comida e restaurantes / Food & Restaurants

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `food-restaurants`
- **Grupo:** Consumo e estilo de vida
- **Subnichos:** fine dining, fast casual, cafes, delivery, chef brands
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 3–7/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `conversion-and-authenticity`

## Objetivos frequentes

- create appetite and atmosphere
- make menu and booking easy
- tell ingredient or chef story

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- menu
- booking
- location and hours
- ingredients
- chef story
- allergens

## Metáforas de movimento adequadas

- ingredient assembly
- steam and heat
- organic masks
- macro reveals
- service rhythm

## Arquétipos recomendados

- `editorial-storytelling`
- `reservation-flow`
- `menu-explorer`
- `location-conversion`

## Engines e mídia

**Engines:** GSAP for editorial, Motion, photo-first DOM, 3D only for packaging or stylized object

**Mídia:**

- real food photography
- kitchen video
- ingredient stills
- menu in DOM

## Conversão

- reserve table
- order
- view menu
- get directions

## Evitar

- synthetic-looking AI food
- motion hiding prices
- poor text contrast on food images
- long preloaders before booking

- [Noma](https://noma.dk/) — `fine-dining`
- [Eleven Madison Park](https://www.elevenmadisonpark.com/) — `fine-dining`
- [The Fat Duck](https://thefatduck.co.uk/) — `experiential-dining`

Essas URLs são sementes de referência. Antes de usá-las, a skill deve abrir a versão atual,
capturar desktop/mobile, verificar acessibilidade e extrair princípios — nunca copiar branding,
assets, textos, layout exato ou assumir que a tecnologia observada anteriormente permanece igual.
