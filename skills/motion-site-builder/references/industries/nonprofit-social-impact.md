# ONGs e impacto social / Nonprofit & Social Impact

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `nonprofit-social-impact`
- **Grupo:** Setor público, ciência e ecossistemas
- **Subnichos:** charities, environmental NGOs, foundations, advocacy, humanitarian
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 3–7/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `ethics-and-trust`

## Objetivos frequentes

- make impact tangible
- build trust
- drive donation or action
- educate

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- problem and intervention
- impact evidence
- financial transparency
- stories
- ways to act

## Metáforas de movimento adequadas

- impact chain
- before-after
- map of work
- progress over time
- story transitions

## Arquétipos recomendados

- `impact-storytelling`
- `donation-flow`
- `campaign`
- `data-explorer`

## Engines e mídia

**Engines:** DOM/SVG, GSAP selectively, maps, Motion

**Mídia:**

- real field imagery
- impact data
- maps
- stories

## Conversão

- donate
- volunteer
- take action
- learn

## Evitar

- poverty porn
- emotion without evidence
- slow donation flow
- unsubstantiated impact
