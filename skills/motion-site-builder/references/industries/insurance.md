# Seguros / Insurance

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `insurance`
- **Grupo:** Comércio e serviços
- **Subnichos:** home, auto, health, cyber, life
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 1–4/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `regulated-high-trust`

## Objetivos frequentes

- make coverage understandable
- build trust
- enable quote or claims action
- reduce anxiety

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- coverage
- exclusions
- quote
- claims
- pricing factors
- support

## Metáforas de movimento adequadas

- coverage layers
- claim progression
- risk-to-protection
- simple guided steps

## Arquétipos recomendados

- `quote-flow`
- `coverage-explainer`
- `claims-portal`
- `account-management`

## Engines e mídia

**Engines:** DOM/CSS, Motion, SVG illustration, minimal GSAP

**Mídia:**

- clear illustrations
- real forms
- coverage diagrams
- customer support cues

## Conversão

- get quote
- file claim
- compare coverage
- talk to advisor

## Evitar

- cute motion trivializing loss
- hidden exclusions
- long cinematic intros
- dark patterns
