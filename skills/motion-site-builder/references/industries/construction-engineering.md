# Construção e engenharia / Construction & Engineering

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `construction-engineering`
- **Grupo:** Mobilidade, indústria e infraestrutura
- **Subnichos:** heavy equipment, EPC, civil engineering, construction robotics, infrastructure
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 3–7/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `safety-accuracy`

## Objetivos frequentes

- show project capability
- prove safety and delivery
- present equipment
- generate project or dealer leads

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- projects
- capabilities
- safety
- equipment
- geography
- case outcomes

## Metáforas de movimento adequadas

- construction sequence
- site progress
- blueprint-to-built
- machine operation
- infrastructure scale

## Arquétipos recomendados

- `project-portfolio`

- `timeline`
- `lead-generation`

**Engines:** GSAP, maps, Three.js for equipment selectively, DOM data

**Mídia:**

- project photography

- equipment 3D

## Conversão

- request consultation
- find dealer

- unsafe depictions
