# Agricultura e agtech / Agriculture & Agtech

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `agriculture-agtech`
- **Grupo:** Mobilidade, indústria e infraestrutura
- **Subnichos:** farm machinery, crop intelligence, biologicals, vertical farming, supply platforms
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 3–7/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `claims-and-context`

## Objetivos frequentes

- demonstrate yield or efficiency
- explain technology in field context
- support dealer or grower conversion

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- crops and applications
- equipment
- field data
- outcomes
- sustainability
- support

## Metáforas de movimento adequadas

- field scanning
- growth cycle
- machine path
- water and nutrient flow
- season timeline

## Arquétipos recomendados

- `product-explorer`
- `field-data-demo`
- `equipment-configurator`
- `case-study-library`

## Engines e mídia

**Engines:** maps/data viz, GSAP, Three.js for machinery selectively, Motion

**Mídia:**

- field imagery
- equipment models
- crop data
- process diagrams

## Conversão

- request demo
- find dealer
- talk to agronomist
- view solution

## Evitar

- city-tech aesthetic disconnected from growers
- invented yield claims
- heavy animation on low-bandwidth field use
