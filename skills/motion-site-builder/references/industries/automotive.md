# Automotivo / Automotive

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `automotive`
- **Grupo:** Mobilidade, indústria e infraestrutura
- **Subnichos:** luxury vehicles, performance, mass market, commercial vehicles, aftermarket
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 5–9/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `product-accuracy`

## Objetivos frequentes

- reveal product desirability
- compare trims
- show engineering
- drive test-drive or dealer intent

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- exterior and interior
- features
- specifications
- trim comparison
- pricing
- test-drive

## Metáforas de movimento adequadas

- camera along body lines
- light sweeps
- exterior-to-interior
- mechanical precision
- road-flow

## Arquétipos recomendados

- `product-launch`
- `3d-configurator`
- `spec-comparison`
- `dealer-conversion`

## Engines e mídia

**Engines:** GSAP, Three.js/R3F for exact licensed model, Motion for controls

**Mídia:**

- licensed exact 3D model
- high-end photography
- technical diagrams
- interior panoramas

## Conversão

- configure
- book test drive
- find dealer
- compare versions

## Evitar

- generic car for a specific model
- unlicensed vehicle models
- reflections hiding form
- specs hidden behind spectacle
