# Moda e luxo / Fashion & Luxury

## Roteamento

- **ID:** `fashion-luxury`
- **Grupo:** Consumo e estilo de vida
- **Subnichos:** fashion houses, jewelry, watches, streetwear, luxury accessories
- **Intensidade de motion sugerida:** 4–8/10
- **Perfil de risco:** `luxury-brand`

## Objetivos frequentes

- create desire
- present collection
- support product discovery
- express brand world

## Prioridades de conteúdo

- collection
- craftsmanship
- materials
- campaign
- product detail
- store and commerce

## Metáforas de movimento adequadas

- slow editorial reveals
- fabric movement
- mask transitions
- detail zoom
- collection choreography

## Arquétipos recomendados

- `editorial-lookbook`
- `luxury-commerce`
- `collection-launch`
- `craft-storytelling`

## Engines e mídia

**Engines:** GSAP, Motion, photo/video-first, Three.js for product only

**Mídia:**

- campaign photography
- high-quality video
- product macro
- licensed 3D object

## Conversão

- shop collection
- book appointment
- find boutique
- explore craft

## Evitar

- SaaS cards
- fast bouncy motion
- effects competing with product
- compressed media
