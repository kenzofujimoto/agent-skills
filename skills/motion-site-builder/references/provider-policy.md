# Generated-media and browser policy

A visible provider watermark is a hard rejection. Do not crop, hide, blur, paint or inpaint
it. Use an official clean export, switch provider or switch architecture.

Browser automation may search, submit prompts, upload and download, but must pause for login,
2FA, CAPTCHA, unclear terms or unbounded paid-credit approval. Never save account secrets in
the repository. Record provider, mode, prompt, references, dimensions and accept/reject reason.
